# 0003: one knowledge repo per platform

**Date:** 2026-10-11 (inherits the Q-SYS repo decision of 2026-10-07)

## Decision

Platform knowledge gets one repo per platform. This is the DiGiCo one. Personal notes,
open loops and client work stay elsewhere.

## Why

One platform means one version axis, one toolchain and one set of people to share with.
Mixing platforms would outgrow its map and mix unrelated release histories.

## Consequences

- Facts about Dante, Waves, OSC controllers or Q-SYS as seen from a DiGiCo stay here only
  for the DiGiCo side; the other side links across.
- The skeleton (CONVENTIONS, inbox, sessions, decisions, templates) is shared with the
  Q-SYS repo and can become a template repo when a third platform appears.
